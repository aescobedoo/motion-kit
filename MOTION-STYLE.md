# Motion style

## Default character

Minimal, modern, Apple-inspired tech motion. Use a dark full-screen scene, crisp geometry, vivid
neon accents, subtle bloom, and precise movement. Let the relationship or transformation carry the
idea; decoration is secondary.

## Visual language

- Prefer circles, points, lines, arcs, routes, containers, grids, and simple abstract objects.
- Use one main relationship and no more than two or three active groups.
- Keep text short, bold, and condensed. Keep code monospace.
- Use color functionally: white or gray for structure, warm colors for cost or risk, green/cyan for
  value or completion, and yellow for a selected idea or resolution.
- Use glow as a tight halo, never as a large fog.
- Avoid large bounces, dramatic rotations, excessive zooms, decorative glass cards, 3D scenes, and
  crowded dashboards.

## Movement

Enter and exit completely. Start decisively, decelerate into place, and avoid visible overshoot.
When elements share time or direction, animate them in sync. End on a readable resolved state.

## Full-screen default

Use 1080×1920 at 30 fps with a dark radial background. Keep important content above y=1300 when
captions may be added later. Use `CreatividadSustraccion` as the main quality reference.

## Personalizing

Replace this document and `motion.config.json` with the user's colors, type, movement, references,
and exclusions. Preserve output recipes and verification unless the delivery workflow changes.
